About this archive

A tidy shelf for every task.

Instead of scattering files across folders, chats, and cloud drives, I keep all of my coursework here. Each entry records the course, the deadline, the grade, and what I learned while working on it.

The archive grows every week, so the newest assignment always sits at the top. Use the search box or filter by course and semester to jump straight to what you need.
